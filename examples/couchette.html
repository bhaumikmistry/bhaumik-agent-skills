Before you commit

Four things we cannot fix for you.

These are properties of European rail, not gaps in our software. If any of them is a dealbreaker, better to find out here than at 21:40 on a platform in Vienna.

Some tickets cannot exist until 30 or 60 days before departure.

CFR, MÁV, HŽ and several Balkan operators load berths in short windows. We hold the itinerary and issue the moment the window opens, but we cannot conjure inventory that the railway has not created. You are planning, not confirmed, until every leg has a number.

Eleven routes still require a telephone call to a station.

Bucharest–Istanbul, Bar–Belgrade and nine others have no online berth sale of any kind. Someone here calls, holds, and posts you a paper ticket. It costs €14 and it takes up to three working days. It is not instant and we will not pretend it is.

Below the sleeper grade, you share with strangers.

A six-berth couchette is exactly that: six people, one small room, a shared corridor WC. Women-only compartments can be requested on ÖBB, SNCF and Trenitalia, and are honoured when the load allows. Sole occupancy costs roughly €140 more.

A missed connection abroad is a slow problem, not a fast one.

CIV rules oblige the next operator to carry you, and we will argue that on your behalf. But it may mean a daytime train and a hotel, not a re-booked sleeper — there is usually only one a night, and it has already gone.

Asked often

Questions with unflattering answers.

Is this cheaper than booking with ÖBB directly?

No. It is €4 more. If your whole journey is a single Nightjet, book it on nightjet.com and keep the four euros — we would rather tell you that than take it. We earn our fee on journeys that cross operators, which is where their own sites stop working.

Can I get a refund if I cancel?

Under each operator’s own rules, which vary badly. ÖBB Sparschiene and SNCF Prém’s fares are non-refundable. Flexible Nightjet fares refund up to departure minus €10. CFR sleepers refund 50% beyond 24 hours and nothing inside it. We show the applicable rule per leg at checkout, before payment.

What happens if the Romanian leg never opens?

You get every issued leg refunded in full, including our fee, and an email the day we know rather than the week of travel. It happened to 3% of Dacia bookings last winter, mostly around engineering works between Arad and Deva.

Do you handle Interrail seat reservations?

Yes, for the operators on the board above, at the same €4. Night trains are where Interrail passes get awkward: the pass covers the journey, the berth is a compulsory extra of €25–€79, and on the Dacia it can only be bought in Romania.

Can I bring a bicycle?

On Nightjet, European Sleeper and Snälltåget, yes — a reserved bike space is €9–€25 and there are between 6 and 21 per train. On the Dacia and most Italian night trains, only folded and bagged. We will tell you which before you pay, not after.

Tell us where you are going. We will tell you if it is possible.

An honest answer within a day, including “fly, this one is not worth it yet” — which is what we said to 140 people last month.
