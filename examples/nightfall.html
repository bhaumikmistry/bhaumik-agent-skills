Light-trap recording

The moth is on the eggbox at 5am. The record should not die there.

Nightfall is a recording app for people who run light traps. Photograph what came in, get a shortlist of candidates with their odds shown honestly, confirm or overrule it yourself, and send the night's tally to your county recorder in the format they already use.

Coverage · all macro-moths, 340 micros Offline · the garden has no signal Export · iRecord, MapMate, Living Record, CSV

The model is a shortlist, not a determination. It never signs a record. You do — and where nobody can, the record says so and stays honest.

Determination

Three specimens from one night in July. The app behaves differently for each.

Most photo-identification tools return a single answer and a percentage. That is the wrong shape for moths, where a good proportion of the list cannot be settled from a photograph at all. Nightfall returns a ranked candidate set and an explicit decision about whether the set can be collapsed.

Certainty

Five states, told apart by fill before they are told apart by colour.

Solid means settled. Hatching means the taxonomy itself will not resolve further from a photograph. The distinction survives greyscale, printing and colour-blindness, because the difference between a species record and an aggregate is not a detail you should have to squint at.

Blocked groups

Groups Nightfall will never split from a photograph.

This list is hard-coded, not learned. Whatever the model's weights say, the app cannot return a single species for anything on it — the answer collapses to the aggregate or to the genus. The list is versioned, published, and argued about in the open with county recorders.

Export

The point of the app is the last step, not the first.

Suggestion is the part that gets demonstrated. Getting a legible, complete, correctly-formatted record into the county recorder's inbox is the part that decides whether any of this reaches the national dataset.

Trap and photograph

Shoot in the garden with no signal. Everything queues locally; time, date and grid reference are stamped at capture.

Shortlist

Candidates ranked, aggregates collapsed, hard groups blocked. Takes about a second per photograph on the phone itself.

You determine

Confirm, overrule, hold at genus, or set aside for dissection. Nothing leaves the phone unsigned.

To the recorder

One tally per night, in your county's format, with the determination basis on every row so it can be filtered later.

Fair objections

From recorders who have been doing this since before the model was trained.

“I have run a trap in this garden for thirty-one years. I do not need a computer to tell me it is a Large Yellow Underwing.”

Then the suggestions are the least valuable thing here and the export is the most. Turn suggestion off in settings and Nightfall becomes a notebook that happens to speak MapMate: type the name, it completes from the checklist, the tally builds, the file goes out in your county's columns.

“Apps like this will fill the national dataset with confident rubbish.”

This is the objection we designed around, and it is why every record carries det_basis and det_confidence out to the recorder rather than just a species name. Model-assisted records are flagged permanently and can be filtered, downweighted or rejected wholesale at the county end. A recorder who wants nothing photo-determined can say so and we will honour it.

“A photograph does not show the features that actually separate these species.”

Correct, and it never will. That is why the model is capped at proposing and why the blocked-group list is hard-coded rather than left to the weights. Nightfall's ceiling is a shortlist. Everything above that ceiling is your eyes, your hand lens, or your microscope.

“What happens to my photographs and my grid references?”

Records go where you send them and nowhere else. Full-precision grid references stay on your device unless you choose the precision to share — most recorders send at 1 km and blur nothing else. No dataset is sold, and nothing is submitted on your behalf.

Field trial

Sixty traps, eight vice-counties, one season. Recorders and county recorders both.

We want the people most likely to find this idea suspect. If you keep a card index and have never once wanted an app, you are exactly who the trial is short of.

One email at the start of the season, one at the end. Nothing else.

Records

Exports to iRecord, MapMate CMR, Living Record and plain CSV in your county recorder's own column order.

Taxonomy

Names and numbering follow the Agassiz, Beavan & Heckford checklist. Aggregates follow county recorder practice, not model convenience.

Blocked groups

The hard-coded list is public and versioned. Corrections from recorders are the fastest route into it.

Nightfall is built with county moth recorders, not around them. Records you submit remain yours; the app is a route to the National Moth Recording Scheme, not a destination.
