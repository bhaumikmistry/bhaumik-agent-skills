# Follow-up

How to nudge people who haven't replied, or push for deeper answers.

## When to follow up

- **No reply after 3-5 days** — first nudge
- **No reply after 10-14 days** — second nudge (try different platform)
- **Short/dry answer** — push for more depth
- **Thread going cold mid-interview** — warm revival

## The no-reply nudge

Keep it SHORT. The goal is to make replying feel easy, not guilty.

**Format:**
```
[1-2 line warm opener] +
[Remind them what's pending — don't re-paste the full question] +
[Make it easy: "even two lines is cool"]
```

**Example:**
> "Hey! Just nudging this one back up. Q2 is still there whenever you have a sec. No rush at all — even a few lines is great. 🙂"

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't re-send the entire question (they can scroll up)
- Don't be passive-aggressive ("just following up on my previous email...")
- Don't over-apologize ("sorry to bother you again")
- Don't send more than 2 nudges — if they're silent after 2, let it go

## The "short answer" push

When someone replies but it's surface-level:

**Strategy:** Use humor + their own brand/work as metaphor to ask for more depth.

**Example:**
> "I appreciate the reply but I gotta be honest — you gave me the plain white t-shirt answer and I know there's a [their premium product] in there somewhere. 😄 Would love to hear more — can you think of a specific moment where that happened?"

**Techniques:**
- Ask for a specific moment/story ("can you think of a time when...")
- Quote their exact words back and ask what it feels like
- Rephrase the question with more context about why you're asking
- Combine the push with the next question so they have two options to respond to

## The thread revival (going cold mid-interview)

When it's been 7+ days since their last answer and there's a next question pending:

**Format:**
```
[Acknowledge the gap lightly] +
[Reference something specific from their last answer that you loved] +
[Restate the pending question briefly] +
[Light closer]
```

**Example:**
> "Just poking this thread back to life before it disappears into email limbo. Your last answer was genuinely one of my favorites — '[quote their words].' That line stuck with me. Would love to hear your take on the next one whenever you get a minute. I'll be genuinely sad if this thread dies here — we're just getting to the good stuff. 🙂"

## Platform escalation

If email goes silent, try a different channel:

1. Email (primary) → 2. LinkedIn DM → 3. Twitter/X DM → 4. Instagram DM

When switching platforms:
- Reference the email: "hey, sent you an email a few days ago — might be in spam?"
- Keep it even shorter on DMs
- Don't be weird about it — one cross-platform nudge max

## When to let go

- After 2 nudges with no reply → stop
- If they explicitly say they're busy → respect it, leave door open ("whenever works, no expiry on this")
- If they declined → thank them gracefully, move on
- Never burn a bridge — you might circle back in 6 months
